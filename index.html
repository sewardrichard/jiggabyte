AI Chatbot

MFZ AI Chatbot: Automated Lead Routing & Support System

24/7 intelligent WhatsApp & webchat assistant built on Botpress that automates lead qualification, geographic branch routing, and fair loan officer assignment while answering customer queries from a verified RAG knowledge base.

Overview

Replaced MFZ Zambia's manual head-office WhatsApp operator with an automated, always-on system that can qualify leads, provide accurate answers from verified MFZ policies, and route customers to the right branch or loan officer without human bottlenecks.

Development Approach

  • Designed the conversation and decision flows in Botpress to capture user intent, collect key lead information, and guide customers through province and branch selection.
  • Implemented a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) layer using a curated MFZ knowledge base so the bot only answers from verified internal policies and documentation.
  • Modeled provinces, branches, loan officers, and assignment counters in Botpress Tables to support geographic routing and stateful distribution.
  • Built a fair load-balancing algorithm that assigns new loan leads to the officer at the selected branch with the lowest current assignment count (round-robin style).
  • Configured WhatsApp Cloud API and webchat channels so the bot can operate seamlessly on both mobile and web entry points.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Ensuring the bot stayed compliant and did not share sensitive financial terms like interest rates or repayment amounts.

Solution: Constrained the bot's responses to a verified MFZ knowledge base and explicitly restricted it from answering detailed pricing questions, instead handing off those topics to the assigned human loan officer.

Challenge: Distributing leads fairly across many loan officers while respecting geography and avoiding overload.

Solution: Implemented a stateful assignment counter per officer in Botpress Tables and built logic to always route new leads to the officer with the lowest current assignment at the selected branch.

Outcomes

  • Eliminated the single-operator bottleneck at MFZ head office by automating lead capture, qualification, and routing.
  • Captured every after-hours lead via 24/7 availability, reducing the risk of losing customers to competitors due to delayed responses.
  • Provided standardized, accurate responses for all customers based on MFZ's verified policies and service descriptions.
  • Gave management better visibility into traffic, product interest, and branch/loan officer load patterns via chatbot interaction data.
Audit Automation

AuditFlow - Complete Audit Toolkit Suite

A comprehensive suite of 8 specialized tools to streamline and automate end-to-end UIF COVID TERS audit workflows, cutting cycle times by up to 75%.

Overview

Existing audit workflows, particularly for UIF COVID TERS, were slow, manually intensive, and error-prone. The suite was built to eliminate manual bottlenecks, improve reliability, and ensure full regulatory compliance for complex audit processes.

Development Approach

  • Developed 8 specialized tools to cover all aspects of the audit workflow, including communication (Email/Calls tracking), documentation (Working Papers/Reports), and analytics.
  • Utilized Python and Streamlit to build robust data processing pipelines and interactive UI/dashboards, such as the Audit Report Generator.
  • Implemented automated processes for data consolidation (Weekly Register Populator) and file handling (File Finder & Copier).
  • Integrated IMAP sync and SQLite metrics for real-time tracking of email volumes and performance.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Integrating multiple distinct audit steps into a seamless workflow.

Solution: Designed an integrated suite of 8 tools (Working Paper Generator, Email Tracker, etc.) that work together for complete workflow coverage.

Challenge: Achieving high-speed processing for complex, multi-source audit data.

Solution: Built tools to process workflows 10x faster and reduce audit cycle times by using efficient, automated methods.

Outcomes

  • Reduced audit cycle times and manual effort by up to 75%.
  • Achieved an estimated saving of 50+ hours per week for audit teams.
  • Improved report quality and ensured 100% compliance with built-in checks and audit trails.
